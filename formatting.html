Marvel Hereos

Iron Man

Iron Man is the superhero alter ego of billionaire genius and engineer Tony Stark, who uses high-tech armor to protect the world.

Captain America

Captain America Captain America's story follows Steve Rogers, a frail young man transformed by a secret super-soldier serum into a peak-human hero who fights in World War II and wakes up decades later in the modern world.

Thor

Thor is the Asgardian god of thunder and a powerful protector of Earth.

Deadpool

Deadpool is a popular Marvel Comics antihero known for his talkative humor, fourth-wall breaking, and rapid healing powers.

Spider-Man

Spider-Man story follows Peter Parker, an orphaned high school teenager from Queens, New York, who gains spider-like superpowers after being bitten by a radioactive spider
